Privacy

Privacy notice

Effective and last updated: November 26, 2026

The Korean American Medical Student Association (KAMSA) operates under the Korean American Medical Association (KAMA). KAMSA's national board runs this website (kamsaus.org) and the KAMSA membership program. This notice explains what information we collect, how we use it, who helps us handle it, and the choices you have. It applies to this website, the member portal, and KAMSA emails.

1. What we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address, and password. Your password is stored only as a secure hash by our authentication provider. KAMSA cannot see it.
  • Profile details: school or institution, graduation year, current status (for example, pre-med or medical student), regional chapter, and specialty interests. You may also add a school email, a LinkedIn link, and a profile photo.
  • Membership records: your membership ID, application and approval dates, renewal dates, membership status, your directory-listing choice, and the date you agreed to this notice and confirmed you are 18 or older.
  • Things you send us: anything you submit through our forms or by email, such as chapter proposals, funding requests, newsletter submissions, Research Network applications, and questions.
  • Technical information: when you visit the site, our hosting and security providers automatically receive basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages requested. This is used to deliver the site and protect it from abuse.

We do not ask for your date of birth, Social Security number, payment information, or health information.

2. How we use it

  • To review applications, approve memberships, and manage yearly renewals.
  • To give approved members access to the member portal, member directory, and member-only resources such as the Opportunities Database and KAMSA/KUMAS Research Network materials.
  • To connect you with your regional chapter.
  • To send you membership emails, the KAMSA newsletter, and news about KAMSA programs and events.
  • To keep the site and member portal secure and working.

3. Who can see it

  • KAMSA national board: board members who manage membership can see member records so they can review and approve applications.
  • Chapter leads: your regional chapter's leaders may receive your name, email, school, and class year so they can welcome you and share chapter news. They may use it only for KAMSA purposes.
  • Other members, only if you opt in: if you turn on your directory listing, your profile is shown in the member directory. The directory is visible only to approved, signed-in members. Members may not copy, export, or use directory information for commercial purposes.
  • KAMA: as KAMSA's parent organization, KAMA may receive information when needed to run or oversee KAMSA's programs.
  • Legal reasons: we may disclose information if required by law or to protect the safety of members or others.

We do not sell your information or share it with advertisers. Partners and sponsors do not receive member lists. If a partner event asks you to register, you provide your information directly to that partner, under its own privacy policy.

4. Service providers

We use a small number of outside services to run the website and membership program. They handle information on our behalf and only to provide their service to us:

  • Supabase: member database, account sign-in, and profile photo storage.
  • Vercel: website hosting and the server functions that send membership notifications.
  • Brevo: sending membership emails and the KAMSA newsletter, and keeping our member mailing list.
  • Google: the board's KAMSA email account, contact lists used for member communications, and Google Sheets used for member-only resources such as the Opportunities Database. Our pages also load fonts from Google Fonts.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile: the verification check on sign-up and sign-in that protects against spam and bots.

Our site links to KAMSA channels on YouTube, Instagram, Discord, and KakaoTalk. Those services have their own privacy policies, and what you share there is governed by them, not by this notice.

5. Emails from KAMSA

Approved members receive membership emails (such as approval and renewal reminders) and the KAMSA newsletter. Past newsletter issues are posted on our website. You can unsubscribe from the newsletter and program emails at any time with the link at the bottom of each email, or by emailing us. We may still send essential account emails, such as email confirmation and password resets, while you have an account.

6. Cookies and similar tools

We do not use advertising cookies or third-party analytics. When you sign in, the member portal stores a sign-in session in your browser so you stay logged in. The verification check may also use browser storage to tell people apart from bots. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings. Clearing them will sign you out.

7. How long we keep it

  • If you start an account but never confirm your email, we automatically delete it after 7 days.
  • While your membership is active, we keep your information to run your membership.
  • Memberships renew every year. If you don't renew within 30 days of your renewal date, your membership becomes inactive. We keep inactive records for 2 years so you can reactivate easily. After 2 years of inactivity, we automatically delete your account, profile, profile photo, and mailing-list contact. You can ask us to delete them sooner at any time.
  • Messages and form submissions are kept as long as needed to respond and run the related program.

8. Security

We use access controls so members can see only their own records and, if they opt in, the directory. Board access is limited to the people who manage membership, and connections to the site are encrypted. No system is perfectly secure, but if we learn of a security incident that affects your information, we will notify you as required by law.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Update your profile anytime under My profile in the member portal. You can also update it when you renew each year.
  • Turn your directory listing on or off anytime in the member portal.
  • Request a copy, a correction, or deletion of your information by emailing kamsa.usa@gmail.com with the subject "Privacy request." We'll respond within 30 days. Deleting your information ends your membership.
  • Unsubscribe from newsletters and program emails as described above.

10. Age requirement

KAMSA membership is for students and trainees who are 18 or older. When you apply, you confirm that you meet this requirement. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that we have, we will delete it. If you think a person under 18 has applied, contact us.

11. Changes and contact

If we make significant changes to this notice, we'll update the date at the top. If the changes affect how we use member information, we'll also tell members by email. Questions about this notice or your information: kamsa.usa@gmail.com.
